Testing-Stage Application Notice

This application and its underlying automation workflows are currently in a closed development and testing stage. It is operated strictly for development validation, pipeline optimization, and internal quality evaluation by Masterstroke Technosoft Private Limited. It is not currently deployed as a public multi-tenant software service.

1. Introduction

Masterstroke Technosoft Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our procedures and policies concerning the collection, access, processing, storage, and publishing of content and metadata when connecting to Meta platforms (including Facebook Pages and Instagram Professional/Creator accounts) via our automation workflows orchestrated with Activepieces and artificial intelligence models.

By connecting your Facebook Page or Instagram account to our workflow or participating in our testing phase, you acknowledge and agree to the data collection and usage practices described in this policy.

2. Workflow Overview & Architecture

Our automation system utilizes Activepieces (an open-source workflow automation platform) to orchestrate social media workflows. The workflow performs the following automated cycle:

  1. Content Draft Trigger: Ingestion of planned topics, prompts, or scheduled publishing cues.
  2. AI Text Generation: Synthesis of captions, descriptions, and hashtags using AI language models based on defined business parameters.
  3. Asset Ingestion: Retrieval of authorized uploaded images or graphic banners prepared for publication.
  4. API Dispatch to Meta: Transmitting authenticated publishing commands using Meta Graph APIs to target Facebook Pages and connected Instagram accounts.
  5. Publishing Status Verification: Receiving status callbacks or post IDs from Meta to verify successful delivery.

3. Data Collection, Handling, and Usage

During the operation of this testing-stage automation, we access and handle only the minimum essential data required to perform scheduled publishing:

  • Account & Profile Information: Meta User IDs, Facebook Page IDs, Instagram Business Account IDs, and Page names necessary to target the appropriate destination feed. We do not access or collect personal private messages, friends lists, or unrelated personal profile feeds.
  • Access Tokens: OAuth 2.0 Page and User access tokens generated via Meta Login. These tokens are stored securely and utilized solely for signing authenticated requests to the Meta Graph API.
  • Uploaded Images & Creative Media: Images, graphics, and visual assets provided by account managers or stored in secured cloud repositories intended solely for publishing.
  • AI-Generated Text: Social media post captions, hashtags, and summaries generated during workflow execution.
  • Post Content & Metadata: Publication timestamps, published post identifiers returned by Meta, and error logs generated during automated publishing cycles.

4. Meta API Permissions and Their Purpose

To enable programmatic social media publishing, our application requests specific granular permissions from Meta. Each requested permission is mapped to a strict operational necessity:

Requested Permission Platform Exact Operational Purpose
pages_show_list Facebook Allows the workflow to discover and list the Facebook Pages managed by the authenticated account for selection.
pages_read_engagement Facebook Verifies publishing status and checks post availability after execution.
pages_manage_posts Facebook Grants programmatic capability to create, publish, and schedule posts and media directly to designated Facebook Pages.
instagram_basic Instagram Reads basic account information (ID, handle) for connected Instagram Professional/Business accounts.
instagram_content_publish Instagram Allows automated uploading and publishing of single images, carousels, and accompanying captions to the connected Instagram account.

Note on Usage: We do not utilize Meta permissions to access direct messages (DMs), read private follower profiles, modify account credentials, or track user activity across unrelated services.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Our automation architecture incorporates industry-standard third-party providers. Information is processed across these providers strictly in accordance with their respective privacy and operational standards:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook & Instagram APIs): Receives post captions, media URLs, and API tokens to execute live posts on your authorized pages. Data processed by Meta is subject to the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Activepieces: Serves as the orchestration layer executing the scheduled nodes and integrations. Token credentials and workflow variables are encrypted within the workflow execution environment.
  • AI Providers (e.g., OpenAI, Google Cloud Vertex AI/Gemini, Anthropic): Receives text prompts, stylistic guidelines, or context parameters to generate captions and marketing copy. We do not send proprietary passwords, customer personal identity numbers, or unrelated confidential records to AI models.
  • Hosting & Cloud Infrastructure: Web resources and policy endpoints are hosted on Vercel with HTTPS encryption.

6. Data Security Practices

We employ robust administrative, technical, and physical measures to safeguard information handled by our automation workflows:

  • Token Protection: OAuth access tokens and API keys are stored in encrypted environment variables or secure key vaults with restricted access.
  • Encrypted Transmission: All data transmissions between Activepieces, AI endpoints, and the Meta Graph API are conducted over TLS 1.3/HTTPS encrypted connections.
  • Principle of Least Privilege: Only authorized engineering personnel involved in testing and development maintain administrative access to workflow triggers and logs.

7. Data Retention and Deletion Procedures

Given that this workflow is in an active testing stage, data retention is kept to the absolute operational minimum:

  • Access Tokens: Tokens are retained only for the duration of the testing cycle or until invalidated or manually revoked.
  • Execution Logs: Activepieces execution logs (containing transient payload references) are automatically rotated and purged after 14 to 30 days.
  • Published Media: Once media is successfully posted to Meta, temporary file references on staging storage are deleted within 7 days.

Data Deletion Request Instructions

You have the right to request the immediate erasure of any stored credentials, tokens, or operational logs linked to your accounts. You can initiate data deletion by:

  1. Revoking application permissions at any time via your Facebook account under Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Submitting a written deletion request to support@mstblockchain.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Social Automation". We will process and confirm complete deletion within 5 business days.

  • Lodge inquiries or concerns regarding automated data handling.
